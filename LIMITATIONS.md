# Limitations

- Recommendations are estimates based on the supplied context and reviewed issuer terms.
- Banks determine eligibility from merchant IDs, MCCs, posting dates and internal systems unavailable to this app.
- The single-purchase recommender accepts cap usage in the engine, but the UI does not yet infer issuer statement-cycle boundaries.
- Axis Cashback's highest online rate is presented as a tier maximum; cumulative spend can place a transaction in a lower tier.
- Data is stored in an anonymous temporary workspace. It may disappear when the hosting platform restarts and is not a replacement for a financial record.
- No card numbers, credentials, personally identifying details, application links or affiliate tracking are collected.
